About Me

Hi, my name is Olivia.

I started in marketing and spent 5 years across B2B and B2C, covering content, campaigns, branding and growth. Somewhere along the way, I realised what excited me most was not just marketing the product, but understanding how it worked and the difference it made to the people using it.

A website revamp project changed everything. Taking something old and abandoned and watching it come alive again for real users gave me a sense of fulfilment I hadn't felt before. That's when I understood what drives me: building things that actually matter to the people using them.

I'm actively seeking roles in Product Marketing, looking to bring a marketer's instinct and an AI-enabled mindset to the team.
